Help record hunters find your shop

Wax & Know is an app for people who love vinyl and the stories behind records. We're building it for people who travel and look for record shops wherever they land, but rarely know what a shop carries before they walk in. We want to help them find shops like yours and come through your door.

How it works

Collectors keep a list of the records they're looking for (wanted) and the genre they love. When a record shop has something that matches, Wax & Know points them to it, with a link straight to your shop. They buy from you, in your shop or on your website, never through us.

What we'd show

  • Your records: artist, title, label, your price, your photo, and whether it's sold out.
  • Your shop: name and logo, address with a map and directions, opening hours, and links to your website and Instagram.

What it costs you

Nothing, it’s FREE now and always.

The terms

By replying "yes" to our email or Instagram message, your shop ("you") gives Wax & Know ("we") permission as follows:

  1. What we show. For records you list for sale, we may show the artist, title, label, price, your product photo and availability (including "sold out") in the Wax & Know app.
  2. How we get it. We may read your public shop pages once a week, or use a file you send us (for example a CSV or a shared drive). We will never access anything that needs a login.
  3. Link to you. Every record we show links to your shop: the record's own page where we can, otherwise your shop's home page.
  4. Photo credit. Each of your photos is shown with "Photo courtesy of your shop".
  5. Prices and availability. We show each price labelled "price on 20 October 2026, check with the shop". We do not sell anything or take payment for your records.
  6. Cost. FREE for you, now and always. We will never charge you for this listing.
  7. Removal. You can ask us at any time, by email or Instagram message, to change or remove any or all of your records. We will do it, usually within 7 days and never more than 14, and stop checking your shop.
  8. About us. Wax & Know is a solo record lover project. I’m building this app as I always struggled to find record shops while traveling or if a record I have been chasing for months shows up in my hometown recordshop.

If we ever change these terms, we'll post a new version with a new date and tell you before it applies to your shop. Until you agree to the new version, the one you said yes to stays in place.

Ready to join?

Just reply "yes" to our email or Instagram message. Questions, changes or removal: hello@waxandknow.com or @waxandknow on Instagram.
